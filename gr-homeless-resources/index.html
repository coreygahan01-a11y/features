Shelter & Housing

Michigan 2-1-1 (Housing & Shelter Intake)

24/7 free, confidential referrals for emergency shelter beds, warming/cooling centers, eviction prevention, and utility assistance across Kent County.

  • Phone: 2-1-1 or 1-800-887-1107
  • Website: mi211.org
Shelter & Housing

Local Emergency Shelters & Outreach

Connect through 2-1-1 or Kent County Homeless Continuum of Care (Coalition to End Homelessness) for current bed availability, family shelter, and street outreach teams.

  • Directory: Coalition to End Homelessness
  • Intake: Call 2-1-1 before traveling when possible
Food & Meals

Community Meals & Food Pantries

Daily hot meals, mobile food pantries, and emergency grocery boxes are available across Grand Rapids neighborhoods.

  • Food Locator: Feeding America West Michigan
  • Phone: Call 2-1-1 for today’s meal schedule
Health & Crisis

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential 24/7 mental health, emotional distress, and substance-use crisis support by call or text in English and Spanish.

  • Call or Text: 988
  • Website: 988lifeline.org
Health & Crisis

Free & Low-Cost Medical Clinics

Walk-in and sliding-scale primary care, prescriptions, dental, and behavioral health services for uninsured or unhoused community members.

  • Finder: HRSA Health Center Finder
  • Tip: Ask for a community health worker for ID/Medicaid help
Day Centers & Aid

Day Centers, Mail, IDs & Showers

Daytime drop-in spaces offering restrooms, showers, laundry, phone charging, mail pickup, and help replacing Michigan state IDs or birth certificates.

  • Referrals: Call 2-1-1 for current drop-in hours
  • Note: Update this card with your preferred local partner hours

Veterans Housing & Legal Aid

Dedicated housing navigation, benefits assistance, and free civil legal aid for veterans and residents facing eviction or record barriers.

  • VA Homeless Line: 1-877-424-3838 (24/7)
  • Legal Help: michiganlegalhelp.org
Volunteer & Donate

How Neighbors Can Help

Support Grand Rapids outreach groups by donating seasonal gear (coats, socks, hygiene kits), volunteering at meal programs, or funding rapid re-housing efforts.

  • Volunteer Hub: Heart of West Michigan United Way
  • Action: Check current most-needed items before dropping off
